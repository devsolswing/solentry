Edit bot

Bots are built by builderBot — it asks simple questions one at a time and turns your answers into the bot's rules. To build a new bot, press Create Bot on the Bots page. This page only changes the limits below; the trading rules themselves are set through builderBot or the assistant, which always shows them back and asks you to confirm.

Silent is off by default. When it is on, the bot only trades within your limits (buy size, stop loss, max open trades) and never more than your balance. Every trade is logged on your dashboard, and you can turn it off any time. Changing this asks you to confirm first.
